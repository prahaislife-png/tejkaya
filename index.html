The house

Heritage, without nostalgia as costume.

Indian botanical knowledge is not a moodboard. It is a lineage of kitchens, courtyards, grinding stones and physicians. Tej Kaya borrows the discipline of that lineage and refuses the visual clichés that have cheapened it.

Gold is used as a quiet metal, not a sticker. Green glass and terracotta sit beside cream ceramic. Sanskrit appears as a whisper of identity, never as decoration pasted on a white label.
